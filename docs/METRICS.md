## Success Criteria by Phase

### Pilot (4 stores)
- 90% activation
- 70% DAU
- < 60 sec time-to-answer
- NPS > 20

### Regional (50 stores)
- 85% activation
- 80% weekly retention
- < 30 sec time-to-answer

### Full Deployment
- 80% company-wide DAU
- 95% inventory accuracy
- Measurable sales impact
